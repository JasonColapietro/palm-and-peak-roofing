Roof inspections

Understand what is happening above your ceiling, with a careful look at the visible condition of your roof.

Clarity before the next decision.

An inspection brings the conversation back to the roof itself. It helps organize visible concerns, identify areas for closer review, and make the next conversation more useful.

  • Roof covering

    Review visible wear, damaged areas, and the overall condition of the roof surface.

  • Edges and roof details

    Look at accessible flashing, penetrations, and transitions where different parts of the roof meet.

  • Drainage and signs of moisture

    Review accessible drainage areas and discuss any stains, leaks, or moisture concerns you have noticed.

What the process looks like.

A shared understanding of the roof, the options, and the work ahead.

01

Share what you have noticed

Describe recent weather, visible changes, leaks, and any previous roof work.

02

Review accessible areas

Examine the visible roof condition and document areas that need attention.

03

Discuss the findings

Walk through the observations and identify which questions or repairs need a closer look.

A few common questions.

When might an inspection be useful?

Common reasons include a visible change in the roof, a new leak or stain, recent severe weather, or planning for future maintenance or replacement.

Will every part of the roof be visible?

Access and visibility vary by property. Concealed areas and unsafe access can limit what an inspection can establish; those limitations should be explained.

What happens after the inspection?

The next step is a discussion of the visible findings, any limitations, and whether maintenance, further investigation, or repair planning is appropriate.
