Storm restoration

A clear path from post-storm questions to a considered repair plan for your home.

Start with the condition. Then make a plan.

After severe weather, it helps to understand the visible condition of the roof before deciding on the scope of work. Assessment, documentation, and clear priorities give the project a practical starting point.

  • Visible roof damage

    Document observed damage to the roof covering and accessible roof details.

  • Leaks and affected areas

    Discuss the changes you have noticed inside and outside the home, along with any immediate concerns.

  • Repair scope

    Identify the areas that need attention and the questions to resolve before a repair plan is agreed.

What the process looks like.

A shared understanding of the roof, the options, and the work ahead.

01

Assess the property

Begin with reported concerns and a review of accessible roof areas.

02

Document the observations

Organize photos and notes so the condition and proposed work can be discussed clearly.

03

Agree on the next steps

Set out the repair scope, project sequence, and details that need further review.

A few common questions.

Does storm damage always mean replacing the roof?

The scope depends on the condition and extent of the damage. A review of the roof helps frame the discussion about repair or replacement.

What information is useful after a storm?

The approximate date, what you noticed, photos taken from a safe location, and records of prior roof work can help explain the concern.

Can I use photos from the ground?

Yes. Share what you can document from a safe location. Avoid climbing onto a roof to collect photos.
